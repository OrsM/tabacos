# Do tobacconists stock up before a tax rise?

The Comisionado reports distributor sales to tobacconists (sell-in), so a rise announced for 1 January
should show as extra sales in December and fewer in January.

**Statistic:** year-on-year growth of December and of January. Stockpiling = high December, low January
(`swing` = December minus January). `net` = growth of the two months together: if it stays near trend, the
effect is timing only.

**Comparison:** the same two months in other years, with no tax change on 1 January, are placebos.
The year after the event is shown but not a placebo: its comparison base holds the stockpile.

**What would change my mind:** placebo swings as large as the event's, or a December/January swing in years
with no tax change that I have not accounted for (calendar, Christmas, price announcements).

**Data limit:** monthly volumes start in 2020 (Jan 2020 report), so yoy exists from 2021 and Dec/Jan swings
from January 2022: three placebo years and one event (1 January 2025). Earlier tax changes
(2012, 2013, 2016) have no monthly volumes.
