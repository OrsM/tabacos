## Reading

- Winter 2024/25: December +11.2%, January -10.8%, a swing of 21.9 points. The two months together changed by -0.1%.
- Placebo winters (3): swings of -6.2, -1.9, -15.5 points.
- Placebo swings are all negative (January stronger than December); the tax winter is the only positive one. But single-month growth is noisy: placebo swings span -15.5 to -1.9 points, so the event stands out in sign and size, not by a wide margin.
- Dec + Jan together: -0.1% in the tax winter against +2.3%, -3.1%, +4.3% in the placebos, so the pull-forward looks like timing, not lost volume.
- Not controlled: working days and the number of weekends in each month, which can move a single month by several points.
- Three placebos and one event is a small sample; this shows the pattern, it does not estimate an effect size.
